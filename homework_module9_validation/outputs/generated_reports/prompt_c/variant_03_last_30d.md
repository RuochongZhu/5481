# Earthquake Situation Report

## Executive Summary
150 seismic events (M4.0–M6.2) were recorded globally between 2026-01-17 and 2026-02-16, with an average magnitude of 4.62. Tobelo, Indonesia led all regions with 19 events, followed by Mohr, Iran with 15 events. The concentration of shallow-focus activity near Tobelo and persistent clustering around Mohr, Iran represent the primary operational signals in this reporting window.

## Top Signals
- **Tobelo, Indonesia recorded 19 events**—the highest regional frequency in the digest—including a M5.7 at 152.9 km depth on 2026-02-14 and a M4.5 at only 3.3 km depth on the same date, indicating both deep and shallow seismic processes are active in this zone.
- **Mohr, Iran produced 15 events**, with sample depths as shallow as 1.9 km (M4.1, 2026-02-12) and 8.5 km (M4.9, 2026-02-12), suggesting near-surface rupture potential in a populated inland region.
- **Nikolski, Alaska contributed 13 events**, including a M5.1 at 14.2 km depth on 2026-02-14 and a M4.7 at 24.7 km on 2026-02-15, sustaining elevated Aleutian arc activity throughout the reporting period.
- **The period's maximum magnitude of M6.2 was recorded in the dataset**, and daily counts spiked to 9 events on both 2026-02-03 and 2026-02-14, indicating episodic clustering rather than uniform background seismicity.

## Risk Watch (Next 7 Days)
- **Tobelo, Indonesia** warrants priority monitoring given 19 events over 30 days and documented shallow-focus events (≤3.3 km depth); coordinators should track whether shallow activity intensifies, as near-surface ruptures in this tectonically complex region carry elevated ground-shaking and secondary-hazard implications.
- **Mohr, Iran** should be watched closely given multiple events at depths under 10 km; shallow crustal seismicity in this region suggests continued stress release that may affect nearby infrastructure, and the 15-event cluster merits sustained seismograph network attention.
- **The M4.0–M6.2 magnitude band across the Aleutian–Alaska arc** (Nikolski: 13 events; Aleutian Islands sample events at M4.6 and M5.2) indicates ongoing subduction-zone loading; monitoring for any magnitude escalation above M6.0 in this corridor is advisable given its tsunami-generation potential, though no tsunami events were flagged in the current digest.

## Data Caveats
- **Data window**: This digest covers a 30-day period from 2026-01-17 through 2026-02-16, queried at 2026-05-11T00:41:44Z; events outside this window are not represented, and the roughly 3-month gap between the data end date and query date means recent seismicity is not captured.
- **Dataset limitations**: The source is identified as "usgs-synthetic," meaning these records may not reflect actual USGS catalog data; the sample events provided (30 records) are a subset of the full 150-event catalog, so depth distributions, magnitude-frequency relationships, and regional breakdowns drawn solely from sample events should be treated as illustrative rather than statistically complete.
