# Earthquake Situation Report

## Executive Summary
45 seismic events were recorded globally between 2026-01-17 and 2026-02-16, with magnitudes ranging from 4.0 to 5.7 and an average of 4.63. Tobelo, Indonesia dominated activity with 8 events, followed by the Mariana Islands region and Kuril'sk, Russia, each logging 4 events. The headline risk signal is a magnitude 5.7 shallow-focus event near the Kermadec Islands on 2026-02-08 that triggered a tsunami flag — the only tsunami-associated event in the dataset.

## Top Signals
- **Tobelo, Indonesia** recorded 8 events over the 30-day window, the highest regional frequency in the dataset, including the period's co-equal maximum magnitude of 5.7 at 152.9 km depth on 2026-02-14 and a shallower M5.3 at 21.4 km depth on 2026-02-04, indicating sustained seismic loading in this area.
- **Kermadec Islands** produced the dataset's only tsunami-flagged event: a M5.7 at just 5.7 km depth on 2026-02-08, underscoring the tsunami generation potential of shallow, high-magnitude events in this subduction zone.
- **Mariana Islands region** contributed 4 events ranging from M4.3 to M5.7, with the M5.7 at 26.5 km depth on 2026-01-28 representing one of three events in the dataset at the maximum recorded magnitude.
- **Mohr, Iran** registered 3 events, including an M4.9 at 89.1 km depth on 2026-02-04, placing a continental interior zone among the top-5 most active regions — notable given Iran's history of destructive inland seismicity.

## Risk Watch (Next 7 Days)
- **Tobelo, Indonesia** warrants priority monitoring given 8 events in 30 days and a cluster of activity through mid-February; the combination of shallow events (as low as 20.2 km) and deeper intermediate events suggests a complex fault environment where stress transfer to shallower structures merits close attention.
- **Kermadec Islands region** should be kept under continuous tsunami-alert watch; the confirmed tsunami flag on the 2026-02-08 M5.7 at 5.7 km depth indicates this zone's capacity to generate sea-level disturbances even at moderate magnitudes, and any renewed shallow activity in this band warrants immediate coastal notification review.
- **Mariana Islands region and Kuril'sk, Russia** (4 events each) represent elevated-frequency western Pacific arc segments where the M5.4–M5.7 magnitude band has already been reached; continued activity in the M4.5–M5.7 range across these arcs should be tracked for clustering patterns that may indicate mainshock-aftershock sequences.

## Data Caveats
- This digest covers a 30-day window from 2026-01-17 to 2026-02-16 and represents a 30% subsample (subsample_frac: 0.3, RNG seed: 102) of the underlying USGS-synthetic catalog; true event counts and regional frequencies for the full catalog are approximately 3.3× higher than figures reported here.
- The dataset is labeled "usgs-synthetic," meaning these records may not reflect actual observed seismicity; additionally, the digest contains no depth-of-burial uncertainty values, no magnitude uncertainty ranges, and no station-coverage metadata, limiting confidence in precise depth and magnitude characterizations for individual events.
