# Earthquake Situation Report

## Executive Summary
79 seismic events of magnitude 4.5 or greater were recorded between 2026-01-17 and 2026-02-16, averaging a magnitude of 5.01 across all events. Tobelo, Indonesia led all regions with 11 events, followed by Nikolski, Alaska with 9 events. The period's peak magnitude of 6.2 and a confirmed tsunami-flagged event near the Kermadec Islands on 2026-02-08 represent the headline risk signals for this reporting window.

## Top Signals
- **Tobelo, Indonesia** recorded 11 events — the highest regional frequency in the digest — including a magnitude 5.7 event on 2026-02-14 at 152.9 km depth and multiple shallow events (as shallow as 3.3 km depth), suggesting a seismically active zone with both deep and near-surface rupture occurring concurrently.
- **Nikolski, Alaska** produced 9 events, with depths ranging from 1.4 km to 146.2 km, indicating a structurally complex subduction environment; the 2026-02-15 magnitude 5.2 event 65 km east of the Aleutian Islands extended activity beyond the immediate Nikolski area.
- A magnitude 5.7 event 55 km east of the **Kermadec Islands** on 2026-02-08 at 5.7 km depth is the only event in the digest flagged **tsunami: true**, distinguishing it operationally from all other recorded events in this period.
- **Mohr, Iran** registered 7 events, including a magnitude 4.9 at only 8.5 km depth on 2026-02-12, indicating shallow crustal seismicity in a populated continental interior region where ground-shaking hazard to surface infrastructure is elevated relative to deeper events.

## Risk Watch (Next 7 Days)
- **Tobelo, Indonesia and surrounding Maluku region** warrants priority monitoring given 11 events across the full data window, with recent activity including a magnitude 5.7 on 2026-02-14 and multiple events at depths shallower than 25 km; shallow events in this arc setting suggest continued aftershock or swarm potential that coordinators should track against local infrastructure vulnerability.
- **Nikolski, Alaska / Aleutian Islands corridor** should be monitored for any escalation toward or above the magnitude 6.0 threshold, given sustained multi-depth activity across 9 recorded events; the shallow event at 1.4 km depth on 2026-02-05 indicates near-surface strain release that may indicate ongoing fault stress in the region.
- **Kermadec Islands zone** merits continued tsunami-alert readiness monitoring: the only tsunami-flagged event in the digest (magnitude 5.7, depth 5.7 km, 2026-02-08) occurred in a known tsunamigenic subduction setting, and any recurrence at comparable or greater magnitude in this zone warrants immediate reassessment of coastal warning protocols for adjacent Pacific territories.

## Data Caveats
- The digest covers a 31-day window from 2026-01-17 through 2026-02-16, with a minimum magnitude filter of 4.5 applied; events below M4.5 are entirely absent from this dataset and regional swarm activity at lower magnitudes cannot be assessed from this source.
- The dataset is labeled **usgs-synthetic** (variant_id: variant_05_mag_ge_45), which indicates this is a synthetic data product rather than a verified real-time USGS feed; coordinators should cross-reference against authoritative real-time USGS or regional seismic network feeds before making operational decisions.
