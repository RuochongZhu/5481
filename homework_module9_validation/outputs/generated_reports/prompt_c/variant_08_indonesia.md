# Earthquake Situation Report

## Executive Summary
27 seismic events were recorded across Indonesia between 2026-01-17 and 2026-02-16, with Tobelo leading activity at 19 events and Tual accounting for the remaining 8. The headline risk signal is a magnitude 5.5 shallow-focus event near Tual on 2026-01-27 that carried a tsunami flag, combined with a cluster of 4 events on 2026-02-14 in the Tobelo area including a magnitude 5.7 — the period's maximum.

## Top Signals
- **Tobelo dominates seismic activity**: 19 of 27 total events (70%) were attributed to the Tobelo region, with magnitudes ranging from 4.0 to 5.7 and depths spanning from 3.3 km to 195.8 km, indicating activity across multiple crustal levels.
- **Peak single-day activity on 2026-02-14**: 4 events occurred on this date, including the period's largest event — a magnitude 5.7 at 152.9 km depth near Tobelo — suggesting a notable episodic burst late in the monitoring window.
- **Tsunami flag triggered near Tual**: The magnitude 5.5 event on 2026-01-27 at 30 km E of Tual, with a shallow depth of only 5.4 km, is the sole event in the digest carrying a tsunami flag and warrants continued coastal attention.
- **Shallow, moderate-magnitude events in both regions**: Multiple events recorded at depths under 10 km — including a magnitude 4.5 at 3.3 km near Tobelo on 2026-02-14 and a magnitude 4.8 at 2.0 km near Tual on the same date — indicate near-surface rupture potential with elevated ground-shaking impact relative to their magnitudes.

## Risk Watch (Next 7 Days)
- **Tobelo coastal and near-coastal zone**: The concentration of 19 events, including two magnitude 5.3 events (2026-01-31 and 2026-02-04) and the magnitude 5.7 on 2026-02-14, indicates sustained seismic loading in this zone; coordinators should maintain heightened monitoring for further moderate-to-strong events in the M4.5–M5.7 band.
- **Tual shallow-seismicity corridor**: The magnitude 5.5 tsunami-flagged event at 5.4 km depth on 2026-01-27, combined with additional shallow events (e.g., magnitude 4.8 at 2.0 km on 2026-02-14), suggests the shallow fault structures near Tual merit priority watch for any repeat shallow ruptures that could reactivate tsunami-generation conditions.
- **Late-period activity acceleration near Tobelo**: Daily event counts show a recurrence of multi-event days (4 events on 2026-02-14, 2 events on 2026-01-31 and 2026-01-27), suggesting the Tobelo zone's seismicity rate has not subsided; the M4.0–M5.7 magnitude band should be the primary monitoring focus through the next reporting window.

## Data Caveats
- **Data window**: This digest covers a 31-day period from 2026-01-17 to 2026-02-16, filtered exclusively to Indonesia via a region-substring query; events outside this geographic filter or time window are not represented.
- **Dataset limitations**: The source is labeled "usgs-synthetic," which may indicate a modeled or simulated dataset rather than verified observational catalog data; depth precision, tsunami flags, and magnitude values should be cross-referenced against authoritative real-time feeds (e.g., BMKG, USGS ComCat) before driving resource-deployment decisions. Additionally, the digest provides only 27 sample events, and it is not derivable from current data whether this constitutes the complete event set or a subset.
