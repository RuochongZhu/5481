# Earthquake Situation Report

## Executive Summary
38 seismic events were recorded globally between 2026-02-09 and 2026-02-15, ranging from magnitude 4.0 to 5.7 and averaging magnitude 4.56. Tobelo, Indonesia led all regions with 5 events, followed by Nikolski, Alaska with 4 events. A notable late-period acceleration — 9 events on 2026-02-14 alone — combined with a magnitude 5.7 strike near Tobelo indicates elevated activity warranting close monitoring in the western Pacific and Aleutian arc.

- **Nikolski, Alaska** produced 4 events, including a magnitude 5.1 at 14.2 km depth on 2026-02-14 and a magnitude 4.7 at 24.7 km depth on 2026-02-15, indicating sustained shallow seismicity along the Aleutian arc.
- **Daily event counts accelerated sharply**, rising from 2 events on 2026-02-11 to a peak of 9 events on 2026-02-14, before partially subsiding to 6 on 2026-02-15 — a trend that suggests a clustering episode rather than uniform background activity.
- **Mohr, Iran** registered 3 events between 2026-02-12 and 2026-02-13, including a magnitude 4.9 at shallow depth (8.5 km) and a magnitude 4.1 at near-surface depth (1.9 km), which indicates shallow crustal stress release with potential for localized ground damage.

## Risk Watch (Next 7 Days)
- **Tobelo, Indonesia** warrants priority monitoring given 5 recorded events including the period's maximum magnitude (5.7) and the presence of both very shallow (3.3 km) and intermediate-depth events — a depth distribution that suggests multiple active fault structures in the region.
- **Nikolski, Alaska and the broader Aleutian arc** should be tracked for continued shallow seismicity; the magnitude 5.1 at 14.2 km depth on 2026-02-14 and the pattern of 4 events across the reporting window indicate an active segment that could sustain further moderate-magnitude activity.
- **Mohr, Iran** merits monitoring given 3 events clustered within approximately 24 hours (2026-02-12 to 2026-02-13), with the shallowest event at 1.9 km depth — shallow sequences in populated continental regions carry elevated potential for felt shaking and infrastructure impact even at moderate magnitudes.

## Data Caveats
- The digest covers a 7-day window from 2026-02-09 through 2026-02-15 (queried 2026-05-11), and includes only events at or above magnitude 4.0; smaller events that may indicate swarm behavior or foreshock sequences are not captured in this dataset.
- The source is identified as "usgs-synthetic," which indicates this digest may not represent verified operational USGS data; coordinators should cross-reference against the authoritative USGS Earthquake Hazards Program feed before making resource-allocation decisions. Tsunami warning status is recorded as false for all sampled events, but the digest does not confirm whether formal tsunami assessment was conducted by the relevant warning centers.
