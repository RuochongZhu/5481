## Earthquake Activity Summary: Jan 26 – Feb 16, 2026

- **106 earthquakes** were recorded over the 21-day window, averaging roughly **5 events per day**, with the busiest days falling on **Feb 3 and Feb 14** (9 events each), suggesting episodic clustering rather than a steady background rate.

- **Magnitudes ranged from M4.0 to M6.1**, with an average of M4.64 — a relatively moderate baseline, though the M6.1 peak warrants attention as it sits at a threshold where surface damage and aftershock sequences become more likely.

- **Tobelo, Indonesia led all regions with 12 events**, including a notable M5.7 on Feb 14 at moderate depth (153 km). The cluster's mix of shallow and deep events points to complex subduction dynamics in the North Maluku zone.

- **Mohr, Iran stands out as a high-risk signal**: 10 events concentrated in a seismically sensitive continental region, with several recorded at very shallow depths (under 10 km) — shallow crustal quakes typically cause disproportionately greater ground shaking and infrastructure damage.

- **No tsunami warnings were triggered** across any of the 106 events, including the deeper Alaskan and Indonesian quakes, though the shallow M5.2 off Honshu, Japan (2.7 km depth) and shallow Solomon Islands activity are the types of events that merit ongoing coastal monitoring.

- **Watch closely in the coming days:** The late-period uptick (9 events on Feb 14, 6 on Feb 15) may indicate building stress release or an evolving sequence — particularly in **Tobelo, Nikolski (Alaska), and Mohr (Iran)**, all of which showed renewed activity in the final days of the reporting period.
