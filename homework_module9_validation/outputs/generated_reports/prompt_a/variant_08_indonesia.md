## Indonesia Seismic Activity Summary — 17 Jan to 16 Feb 2026

- **27 earthquakes** were recorded across Indonesian regions over the 31-day monitoring window, averaging just under one event per day, with the busiest single day being **14 February (4 events)**.

- **Magnitudes ranged from M4.0 to M5.7**, with an average of M4.63 — indicating a sustained pattern of moderate seismicity rather than isolated spikes.

- **Tobelo dominated activity**, accounting for 19 of 27 events (70%), with the remaining 8 centered on **Tual** — both locations in eastern Indonesia's seismically active Maluku region.

- ⚠️ **One tsunami alert was triggered**: a M5.5 shallow-focus quake (depth just 5.4 km) near Tual on **27 January** flagged a tsunami warning — the only such event in the period but a serious high-risk signal given the shallow rupture depth.

- **Several shallow, higher-magnitude events warrant attention**: a M4.8 at only 2.0 km depth near Tual (14 Feb) and a M4.5 at 3.3 km depth near Tobelo (14 Feb) carry elevated ground-shaking and coastal hazard potential despite no tsunami flags.

- **Monitor closely**: continued clustering around Tobelo and Tual, any repeat shallow events (≤10 km depth) exceeding M5.0, and secondary aftershock sequences following the 14 February multi-event day, which may signal evolving fault stress in the region.
